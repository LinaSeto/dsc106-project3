Shape Your Climate Future

Welcome to a choose-your-own-future exploration of Earth’s changing climate. Here, you can compare potential futures, look at how those futures affect you specifically, and discover how different pathways shape the world over time.

  • Select a Climate Indicator: Begin by choosing one facet of our changing climate to explore.
  • Compare Two Emission Scenarios: Use the dropdown menu to compare different climate scenarios; explore specifics by hovering over each option.
  • Travel Through Time: Move the year slider to watch climate predictions from 2015 to 2100.
    • Lock timelines together to compare the same moment in time across scenarios
    • Unlock timelines to compare across years
    • Press play to animate the change in climate over time
  • Explore Local Details: Zoom, pan, and navigate around the globe to investigate local changes or regional patterns.
  • Find Direct Impact on You: Click anywhere on the maps to reveal detailed values and comparison for that location.
  • Imagine Different Futures: Explore different options and combinations of your choosing. Come to understand how our choices today shape the climate of the future!
